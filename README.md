# Standard errors entered as standard deviations: a reanalysis of Luo et al. (2020)

Reanalysis of Luo et al. (2020), "A comparison of electronically-delivered and face to face cognitive behavioural therapies in depressive disorders", eClinicalMedicine 24:100442, together with its addendum (2020), its corrigendum (Sanger et al. 2021) and the letter of concern by Kriston, Liebherz and Koehnen (2022).

Companion repository to a Robustness Report submitted to the Journal of Robustness Reports. Everything in the report, every number, table and the figure, is generated by the scripts in `R/` from the data in `data/`.

## What is in here, in one paragraph

Luo et al. pooled 14 trials of electronic against face-to-face CBT and reported an SMD of -1.73 (later corrected to -0.92, I² 97 to 98%). The quantity they entered as each arm's standard deviation was the standard error of the mean change, which inflates every effect size by roughly the square root of the sample size. Kriston et al. (2022) identified the error and reported -0.20 [-0.44, 0.05] from their own re-extraction. This repository recomputes all four published estimates from the inputs each document prints (all four recompute exactly, but only the letter's inputs are standard deviations), re-extracts the 14 trials from the primary reports, and runs a specification-curve multiverse over data source, effect-size metric, instrument, inclusion set, estimator and the handling of the one trial that reports only change scores. Across 2388 specifications Hedges' g runs from -0.04 to 0.36 (median 0.17, positive favours face-to-face CBT); the confidence interval includes zero in 2376, no interval favours eCBT, and no specification comes within 0.5 of the published -0.92. The corrigendum's correction of one trial (Wright 2005) replaced a correct value with that trial's week-4 change score, an error the letter did not report.

## Sign convention

**Positive favours face-to-face CBT** in every output of scripts 02 to 08 (the eCBT arm has the higher post-test depression score, or less pre-to-post improvement). Script 01 reproduces the published analyses and therefore uses their sign (negative = less change in eCBT). Every output table carries a `sign_convention` column.

The published numbers need care. Luo et al. printed -1.73 and read it as eCBT being more effective. The addendum reinterpreted the same number as less change for eCBT, favouring face-to-face; the corrigendum's -0.92 kept that reading and its forest-plot axis says so. Kriston et al. pool eCBT minus face-to-face, so their positive values favour eCBT. Figures in this repository place each published estimate where its own paper's interpretation puts it.

## Reproduce everything

```r
# from the project root (open luo_reanalysis.Rproj or setwd() here)
source("run_all.R")
```

Then build the report:

```r
setwd("report"); tinytex::latexmk("robustness_report.tex")
```

Requirements: R >= 4.3 with metafor, tidyverse, here, jsonlite, readxl, ggdist, patchwork, png; and metaMultiverse >= 0.3.1 (`remotes::install_github("cyplessen/metaMultiverse")`, doi 10.5281/zenodo.22706036), which pulls meta, bayesmeta, puniform, cowplot and plotly. If metaMultiverse is installed into a separate library, set `LUO_RLIB=/path/to/lib` before running script 03. The report needs a LaTeX distribution with the packages the SciPost JRR class loads (TinyTeX works: tocloft, tocbibind, titlesec, doi, lineno, orcidlink, bibentry, mathdesign, charter, grfext). The RVE estimator needs clubSandwich (install it explicitly; metaMultiverse only suggests it, and without it the 96 RVE specifications are silently dropped).

Script 03 uses a project-local pre/post extension (`R/mm_pre_post_extension.R`); the same functionality is part of metaMultiverse from version 0.3.0 (`compute_pre_post_es()`, `run_pre_post_multiverse()`).

## The multiverse

Which factors: data source (corrigendum Table 1 | Kriston et al. 2022 re-extraction | the re-extraction made here), instrument rule (primary | clinician-rated preferred | self-report preferred | all instruments, via a three-level model or robust variance estimation), inclusion (all 14 | without Choi 2014 | without the two MoodGYM trials | without arms under 20 | without Kalapatapu 2014, a subsample of Mohr 2012). How factors: effect-size metric (post-test SMD | change-score SMD with SD of change imputed at r = 0.3, Kriston et al.'s value, and r = 0.6, the pre-post ICC Luxton 2016 reports | standardised mean change with r in 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9 | adjusted differences where reported), Wright 2005 post-test handling (exclude | derived post mean with the change SD borrowed | change-score SMD for Wright only), Wright time point (endpoint ITT | week-8 completers), estimator (DL, REML, PM, each with and without Hartung-Knapp; three-level and RVE for the all-instruments branch). Specifications that coincide because Wright is not in the study set are counted once.

## Notes for anyone checking the numbers

- The published -1.73, -0.79 and -0.92 reproduce exactly only with RevMan's SMD variance, 1/n1 + 1/n2 + g²/(2(N - 3.94)), and DerSimonian-Laird. metafor's default variance gives -1.75 and -0.93. Script 01 reports both.
- Kriston et al.'s -0.20 [-0.44, 0.05] reproduces exactly from their supplement with DerSimonian-Laird, a change-score SMD at r = 0.3, and RevMan's small-sample correction and variance (script 07).
- The addendum's post-intervention analysis (0.21 [-0.05, 0.46]) does not reproduce from any input set identified here; the attempts are listed in the report's Reproducibility paragraph and in `data/extraction_notes.md`.
- metafor reports I² as τ²/(τ² + s²); the Q-based I² (RevMan) is in column `i2_q`. For REML fits the two differ.
- No authors' reply to Kriston et al. was found (September 2026).
